The SDG Finance Dashboard provides an overview of situation in financing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), tracking metrics such as overall struture of resources, debt sustainability, and fiscal space. It serves as a critical tool for policymakers, investors, and stakeholders to monitor trends, identify gaps, and accelerate progress toward the 2030 Agenda.
